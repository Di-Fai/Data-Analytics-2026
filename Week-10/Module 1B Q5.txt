The three step names are:

1- UnionedProductData
This step combines the two tables:
ContosoOnlineSales and ContosoRetailSales.
It only keeps the Category and Product columns.

2- DistinctRows
This step removes duplicate rows from the combined table.
So if the same Category/Product appears more than once, it keeps only one copy.

3- AddKeyColumn
This step adds a new column called ProductKey.
It creates the ProductKey by joining Category and Product together with a | symbol.
