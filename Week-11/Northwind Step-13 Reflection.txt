This freight percent information could be used to support several important business decisions, especially decisions related to shipping cost, pricing, product strategy, and promotions. In my report, I used the order-level freight amount and spread it across each sales line based on the percentage of the total order quantity that each item represented. This helped me identify which categories, products, and countries had the highest freight percent, such as Seafood as the highest product category, Konbu as the highest product, Argentina as the highest country, Seafood as the highest category within the United States, and Italy as the highest country for Beverages. These findings could help the business decide where shipping costs are affecting profitability the most and whether prices need to be adjusted for certain products or regions. For example, if Seafood or Konbu consistently has a high freight percent, the company may need to look at product weight, packaging, shipping method, or supplier location to understand why those items are more expensive to ship. The company could also use this information before offering free shipping promotions, because offering free shipping on high-freight products or countries could reduce profit. Overall, this report shows how data analysis can help a business make smarter decisions about pricing, shipping policies, promotions, and cost control while also showing that the calculation method chosen can affect the final results.
